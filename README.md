## 👨‍💻 About me

I'm **Steven**, also known as **Chini** — a Full Stack Developer with a strong focus on **cybersecurity**, currently in my 5th semester of **Information Technology Engineering** at **UIDE** in Loja, Ecuador 🇪🇨.

I build applications end to end: clean interfaces with **React** and **TypeScript**, scalable backends with **Node.js** and **NestJS**, and secure deployments on the cloud. Then I try to break them, because the best way to protect a system is understanding how it gets attacked.

- 🔭 Building **[CodeVote](https://codevote.lat)**, a secure and auditable student voting platform
- 🛡️ Practicing **ethical hacking** on OWASP Juice Shop and **risk assessment** with NIST SP 800-30
- ☁️ Deploying on **AWS, Azure and GCP** with Docker, Nginx, Terraform and CI/CD
- 🌱 Currently leveling up in **backend architecture** and **offensive security**
- 🧟 Off the keyboard: **Resident Evil** and keeping it chill
- ⚡ Fun fact: calm, peaceful and easygoing, until production goes down
